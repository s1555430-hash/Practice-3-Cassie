Through the eyes of a soilder painter

Born in Untermhaus, Germany 1891. Dix lived a simply life with parents being a working class. His mother, Louises Dix, was an art lover who would enjoyed music and poetry. When she could, she would take Dix to an art studio own by her cousin and famous painter Fritz Amann

Dix didnt started his artist training until in 1906-1910, he would be apprenticed under Carl Senff who was another german artist and woul leter becoming a master at traditional techniques, later would enroll in the Kunstgewerbeschule, an school of arts and craft, in Dresden.

After going to the Kunstgewerbeschule the would later go to the Dresden Academy of Fine Arts where he would focus to applied arts under Richard Guhr. with these studies he would use this to apply in his printpainting and paintings throughout his life.

What Came Out of All of This

Political art

The biggest part that took him to draw political art was his trauma in WWI. He first volunteer to join and would start as an machine gunner to the rank of Vizefeldwebel. He doesnt get discharged until he was fatully wounded to the neck. when he returned he started working peices using his trauma and this sketchpad he always carry with him to later make one of his famous peice called :schock troopers advance gas"

What happen to him later

A couple years after the world ended in 1919 Dix would come back to Dresden and created the Dresden Secession, an art group, making him one of the co founders.

After the dresden secession he would aligen himself to an movement called "Neue Sachlichkeit" his art would later be an searing critique during the movement.

he would start and conutiuce his professorship until nazi cames to power in 1933. Nazais would see Dix work as “degenerate,” or something that was harming germany and would bannhis works, leaving him to run and hide and would do religious subjects and landscapes until the year 1969 he passed away.
